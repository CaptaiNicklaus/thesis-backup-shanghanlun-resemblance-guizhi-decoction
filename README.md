## 条文1（核心方）

### Shang Han Lun 第12条 — Guizhi Decoction

**Translation**

In Greater Yang wind-strike, yang is floating and yin is weak.  
When yang floats, heat arises spontaneously;  
when yin is weak, sweating occurs spontaneously.  
There is aversion to cold with a contracting sensation, aversion to wind, gentle fever, nasal sounds, and dry retching.  
**Guizhi Decoction governs.**

**Family Resemblance Note:**  
This passage defines the _prototypical pattern_ of the Guizhi Decoction family — disharmony of nutritive (yin) and defensive (yang), with spontaneous sweating and exterior wind involvement. It functions as the conceptual “center” from which the following variants radiate.


## 条文2（加葛根）

### 第14条 — Guizhi Decoction plus Gegen

**Translation**

In Greater Yang disease, when the nape and upper back are stiff and tense,  
and there is spontaneous sweating with aversion to wind,  
**Guizhi Decoction plus Gegen governs.**

**Family Relation:**  
Retains the core Guizhi pattern (wind, sweating), but extends toward involvement of the Taiyang channels of the neck and back. The addition of _Gegen_ stretches the family resemblance toward muscular rigidity while preserving the center.


## 条文3（加厚朴杏子）

### 第18条 — Guizhi Decoction plus Houpo and Xingzi

**Translation**

When a patient prone to panting experiences an episode,  
Guizhi Decoction with the addition of Houpo and Xingzi is appropriate.

**Family Relation:**  
Here the Guizhi core pattern is complicated by rebellious Lung qi (panting). The added medicinals redirect qi downward. The structure remains recognizably Guizhi, but its functional emphasis shifts toward regulating qi.


## 条文4（加附子）

### 第20条 — Guizhi Decoction plus Fuzi

**Translation**

In Greater Yang disease, after sweating, if there is continuous leakage of sweat,  
aversion to wind, difficult urination, slight contraction of the limbs with difficulty in flexion and extension,  
**Guizhi Decoction plus Fuzi governs.**

**Family Relation:**  
Excessive sweating damages yang. The addition of Fuzi pulls the formula toward warming and restoring yang, marking a movement of the family toward deficiency-cold while preserving the Guizhi harmonizing structure.


## 条文5（去芍药）

### 第21条 — Guizhi Decoction minus Shaoyao

**Translation**

In Greater Yang disease, after purgation,  
if the pulse is rapid and tight and there is fullness in the chest,  
**Guizhi Decoction minus Shaoyao governs.**

**Family Relation:**  
Removing Shaoyao lightens the yin-aspect constraint of the original formula, shifting emphasis toward freeing yang in the chest. This is subtraction within the family — resemblance through reduction.


## 条文6（去芍药加附子）

### 第22条 — Guizhi Decoction minus Shaoyao plus Fuzi

**Translation**

If there is slight cold,  
**Guizhi Decoction minus Shaoyao plus Fuzi governs.**

**Family Relation:**  
This formula inherits the structural reduction (removal of Shaoyao) while further moving toward warming restoration with Fuzi. It occupies a more peripheral, cold-leaning edge of the Guizhi network.


## 条文7（新加汤）

### 第62条 — Newly Augmented Guizhi Decoction

**Translation**

After sweating, if there is generalized body pain and the pulse is deep and slow,  
**the Newly Augmented Guizhi Decoction** — increasing Shaoyao and Shengjiang by one liang each and adding Renshen three liang — governs.

**Family Relation:**  
This version intensifies nourishment and qi support after improper sweating. Rather than simple addition, it represents quantitative modulation within the family — strengthening the interior while maintaining the harmonizing axis.


## 条文8（合方变体）

### 第23条 — Guizhi and Mahuang Decoction (Equal Parts)

**Translation**

In Greater Yang disease, after eight or nine days, if it resembles malaria,  
with alternating fever and aversion to cold, more heat than cold,  
no vomiting, stools tending toward normal,  
occurring two or three times daily:

If the pulse is slightly moderate, recovery is imminent.  
If the pulse is faint and there is aversion to cold, yin and yang are both deficient; one must not induce further sweating, purging, or vomiting.  
If the complexion shows residual heat and the condition has not yet resolved,  
because slight sweating cannot be obtained and the body will itch,  
**Guizhi and Mahuang Decoction (Equal Parts) is appropriate.**

**Family Relation:**  
This is a hybrid node in the network — half Guizhi, half Mahuang — positioned between harmonizing and releasing strategies. It demonstrates that family resemblance is not linear derivation alone but also recombination across formula lineages.
